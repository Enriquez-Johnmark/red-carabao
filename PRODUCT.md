# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

American and international travelers considering a purposeful small-group experience in Coron, Palawan. They want a memorable nature-led day, credible cultural connection, and clear next steps before committing to travel.

## Product Purpose

Red Carabao presents Island Crash and the social-enterprise mission behind it. This client-ready static prototype helps visitors understand the experience and encourages interest in future applications without taking a booking or payment.

## Positioning

Island Crash is a one-day expedition to remote Coron coves where travelers share meals and cultural encounters with Tagbanua host families. The video-led home page makes the place tangible before explaining how travel supports local livelihoods, cultural heritage, youth education, and the Rice Mission.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static Vite + React prototype with no form submission, payments, storage, or booking integration.
- Available routes: home, Island Crash, About Us, and Our Team.
- Use only verified Red Carabao content; do not invent dates, availability, prices, testimonials, biographies, or impact metrics.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the authentic Red Carabao wordmark and public Red Carabao imagery.
- Field-journal expedition character: nature-first, culturally respectful, attentive to detail, and quietly premium.
- The landing-page conversion sequence is understand the experience, then apply for a future expedition.

## Evidence on Hand

- Public Red Carabao home, Island Crash, and About Us pages.
- Verified Island Crash details: one day in Coron, remote coves, Tagbanua culture, private base camp, shared BBQ, and vegetarian options.
- Existing prototype assets and Red Carabao Wix image URLs.

## Product Principles

- Make place and hosts more prominent than generic adventure tropes.
- Keep claims precise and traceable to public evidence.
- Let visitors explore before they encounter the application CTA.
- Keep the interface accessible, calm, and considered on mobile as well as desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks and headings, descriptive image alternatives, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, and sufficient color contrast.
